PART B — Rewrite.txt rules applied (AI pattern removal)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Rule 1 — Burstiness (vary sentence length; no uniform medium sentences)
Rule 2 — Ban AI clichés ("notably", "it is worth noting", "leverages",
          "underscores", "highlights", "in order to", "has been shown",
          "a crucial/pivotal role", "significantly")
Rule 3 — Active voice ("we" not passive constructions)
Rule 4 — Methodological realism (report limitations, not just strengths)
Rule 5 — Unconventional structure (short sentences after long ones)
Rule 6 — Typographical discipline (no decorative em-dashes)

Key fixes applied per section:

  Abstract   : "replicates and extends" → "partially replicates and extends";
               "Reproducing their CV protocol confirms that" → direct;
               "The framework is then extended by" → "We then extend";
               closing sentence restructured.

  §1 Intro   : Triple-enables list condensed; inverted active voice on
               "have increasingly been applied"; "A persistent challenge"
               → "Class imbalance is the structural obstacle";
               "Studies have demonstrated that" fragment fixed.

  §2 Related : "has attracted substantial methodological attention"
               → "is a well-studied problem"; "has gained wide adoption"
               → "is widely used for this purpose".

  §3 Methods : Removed "In machine learning terms, Short Survivors
               constitute…"; mega-sentence in §3.3 split; "This ensures
               that" → direct; "This choice was deliberate:" → direct;
               "brings the AUC–sensitivity decoupling into sharp relief"
               → "shows the consequence directly"; "constitutes a
               clinically serious outcome" → direct; "constitutes this
               work's primary algorithmic extension" → "is the primary
               addition to the original six."

  §4 Results : "consistent with the reference study's findings"
               → "matching the reference study";
               "Three additions change the comparative picture"
               → "Three additions matter."

  §5 Discuss : Fixed duplicate/broken FVC sentence in §5.2.

  §6 Conclus : "consistent with the original study" → "close to the
               reference's ~0.84"; "is a reasonable alternative when
               minimising false negatives" → "is the better choice when
               missing a Short Survivor is the dominant concern";
               three-semicolon list → three short declarative sentences.

Rule 6 — Em-dash audit: 12 decorative --- corrected, 6 legitimate kept,
          6 table N/A placeholders kept.
